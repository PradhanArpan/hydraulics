> [!note] Core Concept
> In 1908, **Heinrich Blasius** obtained the exact similarity solution of the Prandtl boundary layer equations for laminar flow past a flat plate. Approximate polynomial profiles solved via Von Kármán's integral method yield results within 2% to 10% of Blasius's exact solution.

## 1. Blasius Exact Solution vs Approximate Profiles
Blasius transformed the partial differential equations into an ordinary differential equation using the similarity variable $\eta = y \sqrt{\frac{U_\infty}{\nu x}}$:
$$f''' + \frac{1}{2} f f'' = 0$$
Boundary conditions: $f(0) = 0$, $f'(0) = 0$, $f'(\infty) = 1$.

Key exact results:
- Boundary layer thickness: $\delta = \frac{5.0 x}{\sqrt{Re_x}}$
- Displacement thickness: $\delta^* = \frac{1.72 x}{\sqrt{Re_x}}$
- Momentum thickness: $\theta = \frac{0.664 x}{\sqrt{Re_x}}$
- Local skin friction coefficient: $C_{fx} = \frac{\tau_0}{\frac{1}{2}\rho U_\infty^2} = \frac{0.664}{\sqrt{Re_x}}$
- Total average drag coefficient: $C_D = \frac{1.328}{\sqrt{Re_L}}$

## 2. Approximate Cubic Polynomial Profile
Assuming $\frac{u}{U_\infty} = a + b\left(\frac{y}{\delta}\right) + c\left(\frac{y}{\delta}\right)^2 + d\left(\frac{y}{\delta}\right)^3$:
Applying boundary conditions ($u=0$ at $y=0$; $u=U_\infty$ at $y=\delta$; $\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=0$ at $y=\delta$; $\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2}=0$ at $y=0$):
$$\frac{u}{U_\infty} = \frac{3}{2}\left(\frac{y}{\delta}\right) - \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{y}{\delta}\right)^3$$
Yielding:
$$\delta = \frac{4.64 x}{\sqrt{Re_x}}, \quad C_{fx} = \frac{0.646}{\sqrt{Re_x}}, \quad C_D = \frac{1.292}{\sqrt{Re_L}}$$

### Example 4.1: Laminar Boundary Layer on a Flume Plate
**Problem:** A smooth plate $1.2 \text{ m}$ long and $0.6 \text{ m}$ wide is towed in water ($ν = 1.0 \times 10^{-6} \text{ m}^2/\text{s}, \rho = 1000 \text{ kg/m}^3$) at $U_\infty = 0.35 \text{ m/s}$. Using Blasius's solution:
1. Confirm flow is laminar over the entire plate.
2. Calculate boundary layer thickness $\delta$ at the trailing edge.
3. Compute total friction drag force on both sides of the plate.

**Solution:**
1. **Trailing Edge Reynolds Number:**
   $$Re_L = \frac{U_\infty L}{\nu} = \frac{0.35 \times 1.2}{1.0 \times 10^{-6}} = 4.20 \times 10^5$$
   Since $Re_L < 5 \times 10^5$, laminar flow prevails along the entire plate.
2. **Boundary Layer Thickness at $x = 1.2 \text{ m}$:**
   $$\delta = \frac{5.0 L}{\sqrt{Re_L}} = \frac{5.0 \times 1.2}{\sqrt{4.20 \times 10^5}} = \frac{6.0}{648.07} = 0.00926 \text{ m} = 9.26 \text{ mm}$$
3. **Total Drag Force (Both Sides):**
   $$C_D = \frac{1.328}{\sqrt{Re_L}} = \frac{1.328}{648.07} = 2.049 \times 10^{-3}$$
   Total wetted area for both sides: $A = 2 \times (L \times B) = 2 \times (1.2 \times 0.6) = 1.44 \text{ m}^2$.
   $$F_D = C_D \cdot \frac{1}{2}\rho U_\infty^2 A = (2.049 \times 10^{-3}) \times 0.5 \times 1000 \times (0.35)^2 \times 1.44 = 0.181 \text{ N}$$

### Key Takeaways
- Blasius exact formulas: $\delta = 5.0 x / \sqrt{Re_x}$, $\tau_0 = 0.332 \rho U^2 / \sqrt{Re_x}$, $C_D = 1.328 / \sqrt{Re_L}$.
- Growth rate: $\delta \propto \sqrt{x}$, shear stress diminishes downstream as $\tau_0 \propto 1/\sqrt{x}$.
