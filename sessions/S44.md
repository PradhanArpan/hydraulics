> [!note] Core Concept
> **HEC-RAS 2D** (US Army Corps of Engineers) is the international standard software for 2D unsteady river flood routing, dam-break simulations, and stilling basin hydrodynamics. It integrates subgrid bathymetry with either the computationally fast **Diffusive Wave Equation (DWE)** or the fully accurate **Full Momentum Shallow Water Equations (SWE)**.

## 1. HEC-RAS 2D Project Workflow
1. **RAS Mapper Setup:** Import terrain digital elevation model (GeoTIFF LiDAR). Set project spatial reference coordinate system (UTM projection).
2. **2D Flow Area Creation:** Delineate the boundary of the river flume or floodplain.
3. **Breaklines Enforcement:** Trace channel banks, road embankments, and weir crests. HEC-RAS automatically aligns cell faces with breaklines to ensure no artificial leakage occurs.
4. **Mesh Generation:** Specify nominal cell size (e.g., $5 \text{ m} \times 5 \text{ m}$ for floodplain, $1 \text{ m} \times 1 \text{ m}$ for channel). Run geometric preprocessor to build subgrid elevation-volume tables.
5. **Boundary Condition Lines:** Place external boundary condition lines (Inflow hydrograph, Downstream normal depth / rating curve).
6. **Manning's $n$ Layer:** Assign land cover roughness polygons (e.g., concrete channel $n=0.014$, main river $n=0.030$, floodplain vegetation $n=0.060$).
7. **Unsteady Computation Engine:** Select solver:
   - **Diffusive Wave (DWE):** Ignores acceleration terms; highly stable for flat river basins and slow flood spreading; cannot model hydraulic jumps or wave reflections.
   - **Full Momentum (SWE):** Solves complete Saint-Venant equations; captures hydraulic jumps, super-elevations in bends, bridge contractions, and dam breaks.

### Example 44.1: Diffusive Wave vs Full Momentum Selection
**Problem:** A civil engineer must model two hydraulic systems in HEC-RAS 2D:
- **System A:** A broad, flat agricultural floodplain of bed slope $S_0 = 0.0003$ where floodwaters slowly spread over several days with velocities $V < 0.6 \text{ m/s}$.
- **System B:** A dam spillway chute discharging at $12 \text{ m/s}$ into a stilling basin where a hydraulic jump occurs ($Fr_1 = 4.8$).
Recommend the appropriate HEC-RAS 2D equation set for each system and justify your recommendation based on hydrodynamic terms.

**Solution:**
1. **System A (Agricultural Floodplain):**
   - **Recommendation:** **Diffusive Wave Equation (DWE)**.
   - **Justification:** In slow, flat flood spreading, local acceleration $\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}$ and convective acceleration $u \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}$ are tiny compared to gravity and bed friction ($S_0 \approx S_f$). DWE is computationally fast, highly stable, allows larger time steps, and introduces negligible error.
2. **System B (Spillway and Stilling Basin):**
   - **Recommendation:** **Full Momentum Equations (SWE)**.
   - **Justification:** In a spillway chute and stilling basin, convective acceleration $u \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}$ is massive. A hydraulic jump is governed by rapid momentum conservation and dynamic pressure forces that are entirely absent in DWE. Running DWE on System B would fail to produce a hydraulic jump, whereas the Full Momentum solver captures the standing jump, conjugate depths, and velocity shocks accurately.

<!--tab:Summary-->
### Key Takeaways
- HEC-RAS 2D integrates subgrid bathymetry with DWE and Full Momentum solvers.
- DWE is fast for slow flood spreading; Full Momentum is compulsory for rapid transitions and hydraulic jumps.
- Breaklines ensure computational cell faces capture natural levee crests.
