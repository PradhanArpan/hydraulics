> [!note] Core Concept
> On practical engineering surfaces (such as ship hulls, airplane wings, and flume guide plates), the boundary layer begins as laminar at the leading edge and transitions into turbulent downstream. Calculating total skin friction drag requires accounting for this **mixed boundary layer**.

## 1. Local vs Total Drag Coefficients
- **Local Skin Friction Coefficient ($C_{fx}$):**
  $$C_{fx} = \frac{\tau_0(x)}{\frac{1}{2}\rho U_\infty^2}$$
- **Average (Total) Drag Coefficient ($C_D$):**
  $$C_D = \frac{1}{L} \int_0^L C_{fx} \, dx = \frac{F_D}{\frac{1}{2}\rho U_\infty^2 (B \cdot L)}$$

## 2. Mixed Boundary Layer Formulation
Assuming transition occurs sharply at $Re_{cr} = 5 \times 10^5$:
$$C_D = \frac{0.074}{Re_L^{0.2}} - \frac{A}{Re_L}$$
Where the deduction factor $A$ accounts for the laminar leading edge deficit:
$$A = 0.074 Re_{cr}^{0.8} - 1.328 Re_{cr}^{0.5}$$
For $Re_{cr} = 5 \times 10^5$:
$$A \approx 1740$$
Thus, for a mixed boundary layer:
$$C_D = \frac{0.074}{Re_L^{0.2}} - \frac{1740}{Re_L}$$

### Example 7.1: Total Drag Force on a Plate with Mixed Boundary Layer
**Problem:** A flat plate $2.5 \text{ m}$ long and $1.0 \text{ m}$ wide is towed in water at $U_\infty = 3.0 \text{ m/s}$. Kinematic viscosity $ν = 1.0 \times 10^{-6} \text{ m}^2/\text{s}$, density $\rho = 1000 \text{ kg/m}^3$. Transition occurs at $Re_{cr} = 5 \times 10^5$. Calculate total friction drag on both sides.

**Solution:**
1. **Trailing Edge Reynolds Number:**
   $$Re_L = \frac{3.0 \times 2.5}{1.0 \times 10^{-6}} = 7.50 \times 10^6$$
2. **Average Drag Coefficient $C_D$:**
   $$C_D = \frac{0.074}{(7.50 \times 10^6)^{0.2}} - \frac{1740}{7.50 \times 10^6} = \frac{0.074}{24.082} - 0.000232 = 0.003073 - 0.000232 = 0.002841$$
3. **Total Drag Force (Both Sides):**
   Wetted surface area for both sides: $A_{wet} = 2 \times (2.5 \times 1.0) = 5.0 \text{ m}^2$.
   $$F_D = C_D \cdot \frac{1}{2}\rho U_\infty^2 A_{wet} = 0.002841 \times 0.5 \times 1000 \times (3.0)^2 \times 5.0 = 63.92 \text{ N}$$

### Key Takeaways
- Mixed boundary layer formula: $C_D = \frac{0.074}{Re_L^{0.2}} - \frac{1740}{Re_L}$.
- Transition location: $x_{tr} = Re_{cr} \nu / U_\infty$.
- Total drag is calculated by integrating local shear stress over total wetted surface area.
