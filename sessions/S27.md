> [!note] Core Concept
> **Jet propulsion** propels marine craft by intaking water from surrounding seas, pressurizing it with an internal pump, and discharging high-velocity jets rearward. The momentum reaction of the exiting jet drives the vessel forward without exposed propellers, offering superior maneuverability, shallow-draft navigation, and zero cavitation noise.

## 1. Propulsion Mechanics & Efficiency
Let a vessel move forward at speed $u$, discharging a water jet rearward with relative velocity $V$:
- Absolute velocity of jet entering surrounding water: $V_{abs} = V - u$
- Mass flow rate pumped: $\dot{m} = \rho a V$
- Thrust force exerted on ship ($F$):
  $$F = \dot{m} V_{abs} = \rho a V (V - u)$$
- Useful work done per second on vessel:
  $$W = F \cdot u = \rho a V (V - u) u$$

## 2. Energy Input & Propulsive Efficiency
1. **Case 1: Intakes Amidships (Water enters perpendicular to motion):**
   Kinetic energy given to water by internal pump is $E = \frac{1}{2}\dot{m} V^2$:
   $$\eta_p = \frac{W}{W + \frac{1}{2}\dot{m}(V - u)^2} = \frac{2 u (V - u)}{V^2} \implies \eta_{p\_max} = 50.0\% \quad \text{at } u = V/2$$
2. **Case 2: Intakes Facing Ahead (Intakes scoop water facing forward):**
   Water enters with forward relative velocity $u$, saving pump energy:
   $$E_{pump} = \frac{1}{2}\dot{m}(V^2 - u^2)$$
   $$\eta_p = \frac{\dot{m}(V - u)u}{\frac{1}{2}\dot{m}(V^2 - u^2)} = \frac{2 u (V - u)}{(V - u)(V + u)} = \frac{2 u}{V + u}$$
   As $u \to V$, $\eta_p \to 100\%$!

### Example 27.1: Jet Propulsion of a Fast Rescue Boat
**Problem:** A waterjet rescue craft travels at $u = 15.0 \text{ m/s}$ (approx. $29 \text{ knots}$). The internal pump discharges two $120 \text{ mm}$ jets with relative velocity $V = 25.0 \text{ m/s}$. Water intakes face ahead. Calculate:
1. Total mass flow rate pumped.
2. Thrust force propelled.
3. Useful propulsion power (kW).
4. Propulsive efficiency $\eta_p$.

**Solution:**
1. **Jet Area & Mass Flow Rate (2 Nozzles):**
   $$a_{single} = \frac{\pi}{4}(0.120)^2 = 0.01131 \text{ m}^2$$
   $$A_{total} = 2 \times 0.01131 = 0.02262 \text{ m}^2$$
   $$\dot{m} = \rho A_{total} V = 1000 \times 0.02262 \times 25.0 = 565.5 \text{ kg/s}$$
2. **Thrust Force $F$:**
   $$F = \dot{m}(V - u) = 565.5 \times (25.0 - 15.0) = 565.5 \times 10.0 = 5,655 \text{ N} = 5.655 \text{ kN}$$
3. **Propulsive Power $P$:**
   $$P = F \cdot u = 5,655 \times 15.0 = 84,825 \text{ W} = 84.83 \text{ kW}$$
4. **Propulsive Efficiency $\eta_p$:**
   $$\eta_p = \frac{2 u}{V + u} = \frac{2 \times 15.0}{25.0 + 15.0} = \frac{30.0}{40.0} = 0.75 = 75.0\%$$

### Key Takeaways
- Thrust force: $F = \rho a V (V - u)$.
- Amidships intake efficiency: $\eta = 2u(V - u)/V^2$ (max 50%).
- Forward-facing intake efficiency: $\eta = 2u/(V + u)$ (higher efficiency).
