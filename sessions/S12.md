> [!note] Core Concept
> In **uniform flow**, the component of water weight acting down the channel bed slope is in exact dynamic equilibrium with the boundary shear resistance acting along the wetted perimeter. This balance yields the classic resistance equations of **Antoine de Chézy (1775)** and **Robert Manning (1889)**.

## 1. Equilibrium of Forces & Chezy's Equation
Consider a control volume of uniform flow of length $L$, area $A$, bed slope $S_0 = \sin\theta$:
1. Downslope gravity force: $F_g = W \sin\theta = \rho g A L S_0$
2. Total boundary shear force: $F_s = \tau_0 P L$
Equating $F_g = F_s$:
$$\tau_0 = \rho g \left(\frac{A}{P}\right) S_0 = \gamma R S_0$$
Expressing mean shear stress in terms of velocity: $\tau_0 = k \rho V^2$:
$$V = \sqrt{\frac{g}{k}} \sqrt{R S_0} = C \sqrt{R S_0} \quad \text{(Chezy's Formula)}$$

## 2. Manning's Resistance Equation
Manning showed that Chezy's coefficient $C$ depends on hydraulic radius as $C = \frac{1}{n} R^{1/6}$, leading to:
$$V = \frac{1}{n} R^{2/3} S_0^{1/2}$$
Discharge equation:
$$Q = A V = \frac{1}{n} A R^{2/3} S_0^{1/2}$$

## 3. Factors Affecting Manning's $n$ & Cowan's Method
Manning's $n$ is influenced by surface roughness, vegetation, channel irregularity, meanders, and silting/scouring. **Cowan's formula** estimates composite $n$:
$$n = (n_0 + n_1 + n_2 + n_3 + n_4) m_5$$
Where $n_0$ is base material, $n_1$ irregularity, $n_2$ cross-section variation, $n_3$ obstructions, $n_4$ vegetation, and $m_5$ meandering factor.

### Example 12.1: Uniform Flow in a Rectangular Flume
**Problem:** A rectangular concrete canal ($n = 0.015$) has a bed width $b = 4.0 \text{ m}$ and longitudinal slope $S_0 = 1 / 1500$. If the normal depth of flow is $y_0 = 1.50 \text{ m}$, calculate:
1. Mean flow velocity $V$.
2. Total discharge $Q$.
3. Equivalent Chezy coefficient $C$.
4. Average boundary shear stress $\tau_0$.

**Solution:**
1. **Geometric Parameters:**
   - Area: $A = b \times y_0 = 4.0 \times 1.50 = 6.00 \text{ m}^2$
   - Wetted perimeter: $P = b + 2 y_0 = 4.0 + 2(1.50) = 7.00 \text{ m}$
   - Hydraulic radius: $R = A / P = 6.00 / 7.00 = 0.8571 \text{ m}$
   - Bed slope: $S_0 = 1 / 1500 = 0.0006667$
2. **Mean Velocity $V$:**
   $$V = \frac{1}{0.015} (0.8571)^{2/3} (0.0006667)^{1/2} = 66.67 \times 0.9023 \times 0.02582 = 1.553 \text{ m/s}$$
3. **Discharge $Q$:**
   $$Q = A V = 6.00 \times 1.553 = 9.32 \text{ m}^3/\text{s}$$
4. **Chezy Coefficient $C$:**
   $$C = \frac{1}{n} R^{1/6} = \frac{1}{0.015} (0.8571)^{1/6} = 66.67 \times 0.9745 = 64.97 \text{ m}^{1/2}/\text{s}$$
5. **Boundary Shear Stress $\tau_0$:**
   $$\tau_0 = \rho g R S_0 = 1000 \times 9.81 \times 0.8571 \times 0.0006667 = 5.61 \text{ N/m}^2$$

### Key Takeaways
- Dynamic equilibrium: $\tau_0 = \gamma R S_0$.
- Manning equation: $V = \frac{1}{n} R^{2/3} S_0^{1/2}$.
- Chezy relation: $C = \frac{1}{n} R^{1/6}$.
