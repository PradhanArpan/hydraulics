🌊 Fluid Mechanics & Hydraulics Laboratory

15-week laboratory schedule covering 12 practical calibration experiments: V-notch, rectangular notch, ogee weir, broad-crested weir, venturimeter, orifice meter, pipe friction factor, jet vane coefficient, centrifugal pump constant speed test, turbine demonstrations, and 2D/3D numerical models.

📐 3-Stage Hydraulic Engineering Mini-Project

Group capstone bridging theory and practice across three evaluated stages: Stage I (Analytical & Spreadsheet solution), Stage II (Laboratory Flume / Field verification), and Stage III (HEC-RAS 2D / OpenFOAM simulation and 3-way validation).

Unit 1 — Boundary Layer Theory

Days 1–6 · 06 Hours
Unit I · CO1
Boundary Layer Analysis & Drag Mechanics
Assumption and concept of boundary layer theory. Boundary-layer thickness, displacement, momentum and energy thickness, laminar and turbulent boundary layers on a flat plate; Laminar sub-layer, smooth and rough boundaries. Local and average friction coefficients. Separation and Control.

Unit 2 — Open Channel Flow

Days 7–16 · 10 Hours
Unit II · CO2
Open Channel Hydraulics & Non-Uniform Flow
Comparison between open channel and pipe flow. Uniform flow: Chezy & Manning formulae, most economical section, normal depth computation. Non-uniform flow: Specific energy, critical flow, channel transitions, hydraulic jump theory, jump elements and energy dissipation. Measurement of discharge (flumes and weirs). Gradually Varied Flow (GVF) dynamic equation and water surface profile computation.

Unit 3 — Impact of Jets

Days 17–26 · 10 Hours
Unit III · CO3
Impulse-Momentum Principle & Vane Dynamics
Force exerted by fluid jet on stationary and moving plates/vanes: flat vertical, inclined, and symmetrical/unsymmetrical curved plates. Force exerted on a series of flat and curved radial vanes. Inlet and outlet velocity triangles, work done, power transmitted, and hydraulic efficiency calculations. Application to turbine runners.

Unit 4 — Hydraulic Machines

Days 27–37 · 11 Hours
Unit IV · CO4
Turbines & Centrifugal / Reciprocating Pumps
Turbines: Classification (impulse vs reaction), Pelton, Francis, Kaplan. Velocity triangles, work done, efficiencies, specific speed, unit quantities, draft tube theory, main & operating characteristic curves, cavitation. Hydraulic Pumps: Centrifugal pump components, heads, manometric efficiency, minimum starting speed, multi-stage series/parallel operation, system-head curve, NPSH check. Reciprocating pumps.

Unit 5 — Computational Fluid Dynamics

Days 38–45 · 08 Hours
Unit V · CO5
Hydrodynamic Modelling, OpenFOAM & HEC-RAS 2D
Basic governing equations of fluid dynamics (Continuity, Navier-Stokes, RANS). Grid generation, mesh quality metrics. Initial and boundary conditions. Basics of modelling using OpenFOAM through the SimFlow interface (interFoam VOF). Introduction to HEC-RAS 2D Shallow Water Equations. 2D/3D modelling of open channel uniform and non-uniform flows, hydraulic jump, and comparison of experimental data with numerical CFD models.

Hydraulic Engineering Mini-Project

3 Stages · Group of 3–4 Students

Stage I: Analytical Formulation (CIA I — 10 Marks)

Problem definition, governing equations (Manning, momentum, specific energy, or GVF), hydraulic parameters, and analytical / spreadsheet solution. Deliverable: Concept Note & Preliminary Calculation.

Stage II: Experimental / Field Data (CIA III — 10 Marks)

Laboratory flume data, field survey measurements, or 1D HEC-RAS baseline runs. Error analysis and preliminary comparison with Stage I. Deliverable: Data Workbook & Progress Report.

Stage III: 2D/3D Hydrodynamic Model & 3-Way Validation (Overall CIA — 50 Marks)

HEC-RAS 2D model (or 3D OpenFOAM via SimFlow). Rigorous 3-way quantitative comparison: Analytical vs Experimental vs Numerical model. Deliverable: Technical Project Report, Team Presentation, and Viva-Voce.

1Uniform Flow in Rectangular Flume
Manning's Eq, normal depth		Compute \(y_n\) and \(n\) for design discharge and bed slope.Measure flow depth at 3 discharges; back-calculate Manning's \(n\).HEC-RAS 2D model of flume; validate velocity and depth; report % error.
2Economical Trapezoidal Canal Design
Optimal section, uniform flow		Derive economical section for given \(Q\) and slope; compare with rectangular.Build HEC-RAS cross-section model of the canal reach.2D velocity distribution; boundary shear stress comparison across sections.
3Hydraulic Jump in Rectangular Channel
Sequent depth, energy loss		Compute \(y_2\), jump length \(L_j\), and energy dissipation \(\Delta E\) for 3 Froude numbers.Create jump in flume below sluice gate; measure \(y_1, y_2\), and \(L_j\).HEC-RAS 2D (full momentum) simulation; validate jump position and sequent depth.
4Flow over Broad-Crested Weir
Critical depth, discharge rating		Derive theoretical head-discharge relation \(Q = C_d rac{2}{3}\sqrt{2g/3} b H^{3/2}\).Exp 4 lab readings to obtain \(C_d\) vs head curve.HEC-RAS 2D model with weir inline structure; water surface profile validation.
5Flow over Ogee Spillway Crest
Head-discharge, non-uniform flow		Define WES standard spillway crest geometry and theoretical rating.Exp 3 flume measurements for \(C_d\) variation with \(H/H_d\).HEC-RAS 2D head-discharge rating; 3D crest pressures in SimFlow (optional).
6Venturi / Parshall Flume Meter
Channel transition, critical flow		Compute theoretical discharge from throat width and upstream head.Laboratory flume discharge measurements under free and submerged conditions.HEC-RAS 2D simulation of constricted throat and downstream recovery.
7Backwater (M1) Profile Upstream of Weir
GVF, Direct Step Method		Compute M1 water surface profile using Direct Step method spreadsheet.Measure water profile upstream of weir in flume with point gauge.HEC-RAS 1D and 2D models; compare backwater curve extent and depths.
8Drawdown (M2) Profile at Free Overfall
GVF, brink depth		Compute M2 profile and end-depth ratio (\(y_e/y_c pprox 0.715\)).Measure brink depth and upstream profile in tilting flume.HEC-RAS 2D model of overfall; compare brink depth and profile shape.
9Flow over Bed Hump & Contraction
Specific energy, choking condition		Compute critical hump height \(\Delta z_{max}\) and critical width \(b_{min}\).Flume tests for unchoked and choked subcritical flow regimes.HEC-RAS 2D flow field; identify choking and upstream backing depth.
10Manning's n for Different Bed Roughness
Roughness calibration		Estimate \(n\) values for smooth glass/perspex vs gravel/sand bed.Flume trials with two bed linings; back-calculate actual Manning's \(n\).Calibrate Manning's \(n\) in HEC-RAS 2D; check simulated vs measured water surface.
11Flow in Compound Channel (Main + Floodplain)
Divided channel method		Discharge capacity via Divided Channel Method (vertical/horizontal interface).HEC-RAS 1D cross-section model with main channel and floodplains.HEC-RAS 2D model; investigate shear layer and eddy viscosity at floodplain interface.
12Hydraulic Capacity of Road Culvert
Inlet vs outlet control		Compute culvert rating under inlet and outlet control regimes.HEC-RAS culvert hydraulic calculator; headwater vs discharge curves.HEC-RAS 2D model of embankment and culvert barrels; assess overtopping risks.
13Capacity of Bengaluru Urban Stormwater Drain
Urban hydrology & GVF		Bankfull capacity calculation for local rajakaluve section.Field survey of drain cross-section, longitudinal slope, and surface float velocity.HEC-RAS 2D flood routing; delineate bottleneck reaches and inundation areas.
14Hydrodynamics Around Bridge Piers
Afflux, vortex shedding		Theoretical afflux estimation using Yarnell's and d'Aubuisson's equations.Flume test with circular and aerofoil pier models; measure upstream afflux.HEC-RAS 2D velocity vectors around piers (3D OpenFOAM optional); afflux comparison.
15Flow Under Sluice Gate
Contraction coefficient, vena contracta		Calculate theoretical discharge and vena contracta depth \(y_1 = C_c \cdot a\).Measure gate opening, upstream head, and vena contracta depth in flume.HEC-RAS 2D model with inline sluice gate; compare simulated \(C_d\) with lab values.
