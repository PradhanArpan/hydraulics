# CE432P: Hydraulic Engineering Mini-Project
**Format:** Collaborative group project (3–4 students)  
**Objective:** Bridge analytical theory (Stage I), experimental/field testing (Stage II), and numerical CFD/HEC-RAS 2D simulation (Stage III) on a single real-world hydraulic engineering problem.

## 3-Stage Assessment Architecture
```
Stage I (CIA I: 10 Marks)       -->  Stage II (CIA III: 10 Marks)      -->  Stage III (Overall CIA: 50 Marks)
Analytical & Spreadsheet            Laboratory Flume / Field Data           HEC-RAS 2D / OpenFOAM Model
Formulation & Concept Note           Error Analysis & Comparison             3-Way Validation & Viva Defense
```

## Approved Topic Catalog (15 Topics)
1. **Uniform Flow in Rectangular Laboratory Flume:** Manning's equation, normal depth computation vs flume measurements vs HEC-RAS 2D.
2. **Most Economical Trapezoidal Canal Section:** Optimal sizing for irrigation canals, boundary shear stress distribution.
3. **Hydraulic Jump in Rectangular Channel:** Sequent depth calculation, energy dissipation, and stilling basin length.
4. **Flow over Broad-Crested Weir:** Critical depth, head-discharge rating, and modular limit validation.
5. **Flow over Ogee Spillway Crest:** WES standard profile, discharge coefficient $C_d$ variation, and crest pressure distribution.
6. **Venturi / Parshall Flume Meter:** Critical-depth measurement of open channel flow in irrigation distribution networks.
7. **Backwater (M1) Profile Upstream of Weir:** Gradually Varied Flow (GVF) direct step method vs flume water profile vs HEC-RAS 2D.
8. **Drawdown (M2) Profile at Free Overfall:** End-depth ratio, brink depth, and streamline curvature effects.
9. **Flow over Bed Hump & Channel Contraction:** Specific energy, choking limits, and upstream water level backing.
10. **Estimation of Manning's $n$ for Different Bed Roughness:** Sensitivity analysis for smooth vs gravel lining in flumes and canals.
11. **Flow in Compound Channel (Main Channel + Floodplain):** Divided channel method, turbulent shear layer at floodplain interface.
12. **Hydraulic Capacity of Road Culvert:** Inlet vs outlet control capacity curves and overtopping risk assessment.
13. **Capacity of Bengaluru Urban Stormwater Drain (Rajakaluve):** Manning's equation, field survey, and 2D flood inundation routing.
14. **Afflux and Flow Around Bridge Piers:** Yarnell's equation, flume afflux measurement, and 2D vortex shedding visualization.
15. **Flow Under Sluice Gate:** Contraction coefficient $C_c$, vena contracta, and downstream submerged/free jump formation.
