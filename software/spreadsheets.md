# Hydraulics Spreadsheets

Spreadsheet models in Excel and Google Sheets form the bridge between theoretical algebra and automated computation in civil engineering practice.

## Core Templates Implemented
1. **Normal Depth Solver:** Implements the Newton-Raphson method to solve the implicit Manning's equation for trapezoidal, triangular, and circular channels.
2. **Direct Step Method (GVF):** Computes water surface profiles (M1 backwater, M2 drawdown, S1, S2) step-by-step between consecutive flow depths.
3. **Hydraulic Jump Calculator:** Computes sequent depth $y_2$, energy loss $\Delta E$, efficiency, and stilling basin length for rectangular and non-rectangular sections.
4. **Pump & System Matching:** Plots $H_{\text{pump}} = H_0 - A Q^2$ against $H_{\text{sys}} = H_{\text{stat}} + K Q^2$ to locate duty point and check NPSH available.
