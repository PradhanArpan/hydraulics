# About HEC-RAS 2D Hydrodynamic Modeling

The **Hydrologic Engineering Center's River Analysis System (HEC-RAS)** is developed by the US Army Corps of Engineers (USACE) and represents the international benchmark for open channel flow simulation.

## Key Computational Capabilities
- **Subgrid Bathymetry:** HEC-RAS 2D utilizes high-resolution terrain data within coarse computational cells, capturing cross-sectional hydraulic conveyance accurately without excessive cell counts.
- **Shallow Water Equations (SWE):** Solves both the Diffusion Wave approximation and the Full 2D Saint-Venant Shallow Water Equations with turbulent eddy viscosity and Coriolis terms.
- **Hydraulic Structures:** Inline weirs, sluice gates, culverts, bridge piers, and drop structures can be directly coupled within 2D flow areas.

## In This Course
Students use HEC-RAS in **Units 2 and 5**, **Experiments 9 & 10**, and **Mini-Project Stage III** to simulate flume flows, validate critical depth transitions, and compare 2D velocity fields against laboratory measurements.
