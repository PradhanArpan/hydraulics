# L15: Final Viva-Voce & Mini-Project Presentation

> [!note] Laboratory Objective
> **Goal:** Submission of finalized laboratory record, team presentation, and oral defense of Mini-Project Stage III.  
> **Course Outcome:** CO5 · **Duration:** 90 Minutes · **Type:** Hands-on Laboratory Exercise

## 1. Apparatus & Experimental Setup
- Primary experimental apparatus, measuring flumes, piezometers, differential manometers, and pointer gauge calipers.
- Volumetric collecting tank fitted with a piezometer tube and stopwatch for actual discharge measurement.

## 2. Governing Equations
Detailed formula derivations and calibration coefficients:
- For flow measuring notches/weirs:
$$Q_{\text{actual}} = C_d \cdot Q_{\text{theoretical}}$$
- For piezometric head losses in pipe circuits:
$$h_f = \frac{4 f L V^2}{2 g D}$$
- For pump performance:
$$\eta_{\text{overall}} = \frac{\rho g Q H}{P_{\text{input}}}$$

## 3. Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Inspect the apparatus, check oil levels in machines, and ensure zero error on pointer gauges.
2. Start the supply pump and adjust the regulating valve to establish steady flow.
3. Record piezometric heads ($H_1, H_2$) and collect water in the volumetric tank over a measured time $t$ (minimum 30 seconds).
4. Repeat readings across 5–6 distinct discharges covering laminar, transition, and turbulent regimes.
5. Tabulate observation readings, compute actual and theoretical discharges, and plot the required calibration curves.
