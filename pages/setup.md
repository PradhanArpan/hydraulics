# Software Setup Guide

Students in **CE432P Hydraulic Engineering** require the following computational tools for hands-on classroom numerical workshops, laboratory modeling (Experiments 9 & 10), and the 3-stage mini-project.

## 1. US Army Corps of Engineers HEC-RAS (v6.4+ or v6.5)
- **Purpose:** 1D and 2D open channel flow modeling, normal depth computation, water surface profile routing, weir flow, and hydraulic jump simulation.
- **Cost:** Free & Public Domain (developed by the US Army Corps of Engineers Hydrologic Engineering Center).
- **Download:** [HEC-RAS Official Website](https://www.hec.usace.army.mil/software/hec-ras/download.aspx)
- **System Requirements:** Windows 10/11 (64-bit), 8 GB RAM (16 GB recommended for 2D flow areas).

## 2. SimFlow (OpenFOAM GUI)
- **Purpose:** 3D Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) modeling of spillway crests, turbulent boundary layer separation, and Volume of Fluid (VOF) free surface tracking.
- **Cost:** Free Academic / Personal Edition available.
- **Download:** [SimFlow Official Portal](https://sim-flow.com/)
- **Note:** The installer includes a bundled, configured version of OpenFOAM on Windows via MinGW.

## 3. Spreadsheet Tools (Microsoft Excel / Google Sheets)
- **Purpose:** Iterative Newton-Raphson solvers for normal depth $y_n$ in trapezoidal canals, Direct Step Method for Gradually Varied Flow (GVF) backwater profiles, and pump system-head curve intersection plotting.
