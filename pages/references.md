# Prescribed Textbooks & Reference Literature

## Prescribed Textbooks
1. **Modi, P.N. and Seth, S.M.** (2017). *Hydraulics and Fluid Mechanics Including Hydraulics Machines*, Standard Book House, New Delhi, 21st Edition. *(Units I, III, IV, V)*
2. **Bansal, R.K.** (2018). *Fluid Mechanics and Hydraulic Machines*, Laxmi Publications, New Delhi, Revised 9th Edition. *(Units I, II, III, IV)*
3. **Chow, Ven Te** (1993). *Open-Channel Hydraulics*, McGraw-Hill Publishing Company, New Delhi. *(Unit II)*

## Reference Literature & Manuals
- **Chanson, H.** (2004). *The Hydraulics of Open Channel Flow: An Introduction*, Elsevier Butterworth-Heinemann.
- **Subramanya, K.** (2009). *Flow in Open Channels*, Tata McGraw-Hill, 3rd Edition.

- **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS):**
  - IS 2912: Recommendation for liquid flow measurement in open channels by slope-area method.
  - IS 6063: Methods for measurement of flow of water in open channels using standing wave flumes.
  - IS 9108: Liquid flow measurement in open channels using thin plate weirs (V-notch & rectangular).
