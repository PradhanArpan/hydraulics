## Artificial Intelligence (AI) Use Protocol
Generative AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek) are permitted as engineering learning aids, concept clarifiers, and code scaffolders under the following mandatory conditions:
1. **Mandatory AI Use Log:** Every submission incorporating AI assistance must append a formal AI Use Log containing:
   - Specific prompt(s) executed.
   - Raw output generated by the AI model.
   - Manual verification and engineering check performed by the student.
   - Specific mathematical corrections and adjustments applied.
   - Reflection on what was learned.
2. **Accountability:** Unverified, hallucinated, or uncritical AI outputs submitted as engineering design or analysis will be penalized. The student is solely responsible for the technical accuracy of all equations, numbers, and conclusions.
